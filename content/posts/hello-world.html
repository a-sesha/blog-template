This is the smallest useful post: a title, a short header block, a few paragraphs, one figure and a footnote. Open content/posts/hello-world.html next to this page to see how little markup it takes.

{{#draft title="Draft note (only you see this)"}}Anything marked as a draft note is shown while you write and removed from the page source by python build.py --publish. Use it for reminders, outlines and half-sentences.{{/draft}}

What you get

A single column of text at a comfortable 768 px, serif headings, a sans-serif body and a monospace for everything that is data: dates, captions, buttons, diagram labels. Figures can break out to 1000 px, or all the way to the edges of the window.

{{#figure caption="Figure 1. The whole workflow. There is no server, database or JavaScript framework to maintain."}}{{svg pipeline.svg}}{{/figure}}

Three things to change

  1. Open site.json and put in your name, a one-line description and, when you know it, the address of your site (this switches on the feed, the sitemap and canonical links).
  2. Replace the two example posts, or keep them as a reference and mark them draft: true so that --publish leaves them out.
  3. Pick your own colours by editing the variables at the top of theme/style.css; everything else follows them.

Dates and the order of posts come from the front matter1, so you never edit a list by hand.
